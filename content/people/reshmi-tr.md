With a rich professional background spanning 14 years, Reshmi has accumulated diverse experience in industry, academia, and R&D organizations. Her research interests encompass Blockchain, Malware Analysis, IoT, IPv6 co-existence in Next Generation Networks, AI for Cybersecurity, and more.
