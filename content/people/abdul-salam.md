Abdul Salam Mohamed Kani is an engineering director with more than 20 years in financial services technology. He has led engineering across banking and enterprise finance platforms, focused on high-volume data processing, distributed systems, and resilience at scale. His work spans the platform foundations that let large organisations deliver mission-critical software continuously and safely. He now leads efforts at the intersection of enterprise platforms and applied AI, building agentic systems and multi-agent workflows that reshape how software is designed, delivered, and governed — with a particular focus on introducing autonomy into regulated environments without sacrificing reliability or accountability.
