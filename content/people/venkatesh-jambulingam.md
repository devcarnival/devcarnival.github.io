Venkatesh Jambulingam is a Senior Manager in Cybersecurity & cloud security practice leader at EY Global Delivery Services with over 21 years of industry experience across enterprise cybersecurity, application security, cloud security (AWS, Azure, GCP), and enterprise architecture.

He specializes in designing robust zero trust strategy and implementation, blockchain & digital asset security, DevSecOps pipelines, and modern multi-cloud architectures. He is also the founder of Cybervattam (சைபர் வட்டம்), a pioneering initiative dedicated to democratizing cybersecurity education in native Indian languages.
