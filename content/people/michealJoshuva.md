Micheal Joshuva is the Founder & CEO of MICH JOSH CYBERSECURITY Pvt. Ltd. and the Founder of the AI-SEC Community Foundation, a rapidly growing nonprofit community dedicated to advancing AI security and cybersecurity awareness. With over 15 years of experience in cybersecurity, governance, risk, compliance, penetration testing, and AI security, he has worked with leading global organizations, including EY, Infosys, and Wipro, delivering security and compliance initiatives across diverse industries.

He is passionate about building secure AI ecosystems and fostering collaboration between industry leaders, researchers, startups, and students. Through the AI-SEC Community, he organizes conferences, monthly meetups, hackathons, and technical workshops that bring together cybersecurity professionals, AI practitioners, and enterprise decision-makers. Micheal actively advises organizations on AI security, application security, VAPT, and security compliance while championing open-source initiatives and community-driven innovation. His mission is to bridge the gap between AI innovation and cybersecurity, helping organizations adopt emerging technologies securely while nurturing the next generation of cybersecurity talent.
