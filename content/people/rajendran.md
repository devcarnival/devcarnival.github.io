Rajendran Dandapani is a Director at Zoho Corporation and one of the company's founding members, having been part of Zoho's journey since 1997. Over the years, he has played a key role in conceiving, building, and scaling software products that serve millions of users worldwide. His experience spans product development, technology leadership, innovation, and organizational growth.

In addition to his role at Zoho, Rajendran serves as the President of Zoho Schools of Learning, Zoho's pioneering initiative in alternative education. Through this program, he has championed practical, industry-oriented learning models that challenge conventional approaches to education and talent development.

A sought-after speaker and panelist, Rajendran frequently shares insights on technology, product thinking, innovation, education, and the future of work. Inspired by the golden era of Apple under Steve Jobs, he believes in striving to be "insanely great" in every endeavor and encourages teams to pursue excellence through craftsmanship, curiosity, and continuous learning.

Beyond his professional work, Rajendran is an avid reader and lifelong learner. Through his YouTube channel, *Quotidians – Rajendran Dandapani Presents*, he shares reflections and lessons drawn from his personal and professional experiences, offering practical perspectives on learning, leadership, and life.
