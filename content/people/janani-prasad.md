Janani Prasad is an engineering and product strategy leader with over 13 years of experience spanning software engineering, product architecture, and executive technology leadership.

In the UK, she led the core architecture of a supplier data platform that connected over 70,000 small and diverse businesses with global corporate procurement pipelines. She currently leads product strategy at ParserBee, an intelligent document parsing and understanding platform, and is the founder of ChampionHer, an open mentoring initiative for women in technology across Tamil Nadu.

Her industry recognitions include the TechWM Women in Tech Award (2023) and Outstanding Female Entrepreneur of the Year by the Asian Business Chamber of Commerce (2023).
