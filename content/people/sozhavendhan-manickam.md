He specializes in Microsoft Fabric, Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, data architecture, Generative AI, and agentic systems, with proven expertise in building scalable, governed, and automation-driven data platforms.

He has led cross-functional engineering teams, developed reusable enterprise accelerators, and delivered mission-critical solutions. His academic background includes Liverpool John Moores University, IIIT Bangalore, NIT Silchar, and PETIT.
