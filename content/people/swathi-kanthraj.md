Swathi Kanthraj is a cybersecurity executive and engineering leader serving as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Head of IT & Cybersecurity at JazzX AI, with extensive prior leadership experience as Senior Manager of Information Security and Head of Engineering at Amadeus Labs, Senior Application Security Consultant at IBM, and Security Lead at Capgemini.

With deep domain expertise spanning DevSecOps, Application Security (AppSec), Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), Cloud Security, and automated vulnerability management, Swathi specializes in designing secure software development lifecycles (SDLC) and resilient automated remediation pipelines. She actively champions practical security engineering, bridging the gap between developer velocity and enterprise risk mitigation.
