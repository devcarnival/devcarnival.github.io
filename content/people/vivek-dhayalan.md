Vivek Dhayalan is an engineering leader with over 18 years of experience building and running mission-critical production systems across cybersecurity, media streaming, and enterprise data platforms.

He has founded a product engineering firm and led distributed engineering teams building high-throughput microservices, vector search architectures, RAG pipelines, and agentic workflows. His core engineering focus is making non-deterministic AI systems reliable, predictable, and cost-effective when exposed to real users at scale.

Vivek holds an Executive MBA from IIT Madras and an M.Tech in Cyber Security from Amrita University.
