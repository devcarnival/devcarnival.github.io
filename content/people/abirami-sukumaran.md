Abirami Sukumaran is a Staff Developer Advocate at Google Cloud specializing in cloud databases, data architecture, and modern data-to-AI ecosystems. With nearly two decades of software engineering experience spanning Google, IBM, Citrix, and Infosys, she empowers enterprise developers to architect resilient data pipelines and intelligent applications.

Her work focuses on database internals, in-database machine learning, and scalable vector search architectures that enable modern generative AI workloads. She actively champions developer tooling, database optimization, and open data architectures across global developer ecosystems.

Abirami is the author of *Database Design and Modeling with Google Cloud*, co-author of *Integrating Business and AI*, and a frequent keynote speaker at international software engineering and cloud conferences.
