J. Anten Edilbert is an accomplished ad-film maker, documentary filmmaker, cinematographer, and multimedia specialist with over two decades of industry experience. He heads the Media Lab (VR & AR) at the School of Interwoven Arts and Sciences (SIAS), Krea University.

Beyond his university leadership, Anten teaches at the LV Prasad Film & TV Academy and Mohammad Iqbal's Light and Life Academy, and directs commercial campaigns—including high-profile automotive sound design showcases such as the Mahindra Sonic Studio Atmos system developed alongside AR Rahman.
