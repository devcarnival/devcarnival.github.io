Vimalasree Anandhan is a seasoned cybersecurity leader and Head of Information Security at Poshmark India, with over two decades of experience spanning application security, cloud security, DevSecOps, and governance, risk, and compliance (GRC).

She is the President of the NexGen CyberWomen community and the IronLady Chennai Chapter, actively mentoring and empowering women in technology and cybersecurity. A frequent keynote speaker and panelist at industry conferences, Vimalasree is dedicated to fostering resilient product security practices and championing security-by-design for modern AI-driven ecosystems.
