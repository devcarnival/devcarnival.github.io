Baradwaj Raman is a sound engineer, Veena maker, and Carnatic musician. Following in the footsteps of his grandfather, the legendary Veena exponent and filmmaker Dr. S. Balachander (SB), Baradwaj plays the Saraswati Veena and regularly performs across the Carnatic music circuit in Chennai and internationally.

Specializing in the craftsmanship of high-quality Veenas and unconventional music pedagogy, Baradwaj works in the Media Lab at Krea University, bridging classical acoustic heritage with modern sound engineering and visual media.
