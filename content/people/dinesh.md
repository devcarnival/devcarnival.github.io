Dinesh is an educator and practitioner with Zoho Schools of Learning, a pioneering vocational education initiative by Zoho Corporation that focuses on hands-on software development, experiential learning, and practical problem-solving.

He champions alternative, skill-first educational pathways that empower students and faculty to build real-world software products, bridging conventional academic curriculums with rigorous software engineering standards.
