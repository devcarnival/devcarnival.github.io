Arun Prakash M is the Founder & CEO of GUVI (Grab Your Vernacular Imprint), an EdTech initiative incubated at IIT Madras and IIM Ahmedabad. With over 18 years of technology leadership and engineering experience, Arun is a passionate advocate for democratizing developer education in vernacular languages.

Through GUVI, he has empowered millions of learners and established deep ties between academic institutions and high-growth technology companies. Within DevCarnival's Academia initiative, Arun helps drive industry-relevant pedagogy, collaborative coding programs, and developer empowerment for academia across India.
