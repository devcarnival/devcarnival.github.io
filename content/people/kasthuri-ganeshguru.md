Kasthuri Ganeshguru is a Senior Cybersecurity Architect and SIEM leader at Cognizant with extensive expertise spanning Security Information and Event Management (SIEM), Security Operations Center (SOC) design, threat intelligence, and detection engineering.

She serves as a Board Director at the Cybersecurity Council for India and actively advocates for women in cybersecurity as a Program and Innovation Strategist for NexGenCyberWomen (NGCW). Passionate about bridging software engineering and security operations, Kasthuri works closely with engineering and DevOps teams to transform application telemetry into actionable security intelligence and accelerate incident response across modern distributed enterprise environments.
