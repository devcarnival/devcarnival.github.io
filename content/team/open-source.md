The Open Source Team leads the national movement to foster open-source awareness, collaboration, and contribution among professionals and students across India. By establishing dedicated Open Source Labs in academic and research institutions, this team builds hands-on environments for real-world code contributions. Additionally, they curate specialized conference tracks for open-source creators and maintainers to showcase their work, mentor emerging developers, and bridge the gap between grassroots talent and global open-source ecosystems.
