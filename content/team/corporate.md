The Corporate Connect Team is responsible for engaging with companies, startups, and industry partners to build meaningful collaborations. The team works on securing sponsorships, identifying partnership opportunities, and organizing startup-focused events that connect the developer community with the broader technology ecosystem.

By fostering strong industry relationships, the team helps create opportunities for knowledge sharing, innovation, mentorship, and sustainable support for community initiatives.
