Artificial Intelligence is changing not only how software is built but also how products are designed and experienced. Traditional user experience principles are being redefined as systems become conversational, adaptive, and increasingly autonomous. This session explores the emerging discipline of AI-first design, covering topics such as human-AI interaction, trust, transparency, explainability, and designing experiences that effectively balance automation with human control. Attendees will gain practical insights into creating intuitive and responsible AI-powered products.
