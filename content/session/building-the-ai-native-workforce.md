Building an AI-native workforce where employees naturally use AI as part of their daily work, decision-making, problem-solving and collaboration rather than treating AI as a separate tool or occasional resource. The focus is on combining human expertise with AI capabilities to improve productivity, innovation and business outcomes.

### Key Takeaways
- **Human-AI Synergy**: An AI-native workforce is not defined by technology skills alone, but by the ability of people and AI to work together seamlessly to create greater business value.
- **Embedded Daily Workflows**: Transition from viewing AI as an isolated tool to embedding AI into everyday problem-solving, collaboration, and strategic decisions.
- **Continuous AI Fluency**: Organizations that invest in AI fluency, adaptive reskilling, and a culture of continuous learning are best equipped to innovate and compete in the AI-first era.

### Target Audience
- Tech engineers, engineering leaders, and enterprise managers focused on organizational AI adoption and building future-ready teams.

### Prerequisites
- None. Suitable for all technology engineers and leaders.
