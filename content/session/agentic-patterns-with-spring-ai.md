Modern AI applications are evolving beyond simple prompts and responses into intelligent systems capable of reasoning, planning, and executing tasks autonomously. This session introduces Agentic AI patterns using Spring AI and demonstrates how developers can build agents that interact with tools, access enterprise data, coordinate workflows, and collaborate with other agents. Through practical examples, attendees will learn how Spring AI enables the development of scalable, production-ready AI applications for real-world business scenarios.
