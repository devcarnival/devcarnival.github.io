Software development is undergoing its biggest transformation since the rise of the internet. AI-powered tools are changing how requirements are gathered, code is written, tests are generated, systems are maintained, and knowledge is shared across teams. This session explores how developers, architects, and engineering leaders can effectively collaborate with AI to improve productivity, accelerate delivery, and focus on solving higher-value business problems. Participants will learn practical approaches, emerging workflows, and the skills needed to thrive in an AI-assisted development ecosystem.
