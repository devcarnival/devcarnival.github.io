AI process architecture is the redesign of business processes, operating models and technology systems with AI embedded at the core rather than added as an enhancement. An AI-first enterprise integrates AI into decision making, workflows, products and customer experiences enabling processes to continuously learn, adapt and optimize outcomes and using data and intelligent automation.

### Key Takeaways
- **Business Transformation Over Tech Upgrade**: Recognize that becoming an AI-first enterprise is fundamentally a business transformation, not merely a technology upgrade.
- **AI-Embedded Workflows**: Redesign processes around AI-driven intelligence rather than treating AI as an add-on enhancement.
- **Foundation & Governance**: Build strong data and governance foundations while empowering people to work effectively alongside AI.
- **Continuous Value Creation**: Embed AI capabilities to continuously improve decision-making, productivity, innovation, and customer outcomes across the enterprise.

### Target Audience
- Tech engineers, enterprise architects, and technology leaders looking to redesign workflows and systems for AI-first enterprises.

### Prerequisites
- None. Suitable for technology engineers, architects, and IT leaders.
