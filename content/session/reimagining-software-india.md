For more than three decades, India's IT industry has been built on a successful combination of talent, engineering excellence, and global service delivery. However, the rise of AI is reshaping how software is built, deployed, and consumed, creating both disruption and unprecedented opportunities.

This session explores how the traditional software models that defined the industry are evolving and what this transformation means for India. It examines the emergence of AI-native products, intelligent automation, new approaches to software development, and the growing importance of innovation, product thinking, and continuous learning.

Drawing from decades of experience building technology products and nurturing talent, Rajendran Dandapani shares perspectives on how India can move beyond being a technology services powerhouse to becoming a global leader in AI-driven innovation. The session will challenge participants to rethink skills, careers, education, entrepreneurship, and the role of technology in shaping the next chapter of India's growth story.

Whether you are a student, educator, developer, entrepreneur, or technology leader, this keynote offers insights into the opportunities emerging at the intersection of AI, software, and India's future.
