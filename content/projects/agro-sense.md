## Overview

AgroSense AI combines low-cost IoT sensors with on-device computer vision models to provide real-time soil health monitoring and crop disease diagnosis without requiring constant internet connectivity.

### Key Features

- **Edge Computer Vision:** Detects early signs of leaf rust and pest infestations using micro-cameras.
- **Smart Irrigation:** Automated water delivery based on root-zone soil moisture metrics.
- **Offline First:** Local processing node stores up to 30 days of sensor data locally.

### Impact

Tested across 150+ farms, reducing water consumption by **35%** while increasing crop yield stability by **20%**.
