# Tech Pavilion at DevCarnival 2027

### Build. Showcase. Connect.

Have a project you're proud of? Bring it to DevCarnival!

The **Tech Pavilion** is an opportunity for student innovators and developer teams to showcase their projects to the developer community, interact with industry professionals, and share their ideas with a wider audience.

### What You Can Expect

**👥 Team Up**  
Participate as a team of at least **2 members** and collaborate to bring your ideas to life.

**💡 Professional Mentorship**  
Get guidance and mentorship from industry professionals to help refine your ideas and strengthen your projects.

**🎪 Showcase Your Project for 2 Days**  
Get your own stall at DevCarnival to demonstrate your project, interact with attendees, share your work, and receive feedback throughout the **two-day event**.

### Who Can Participate?

Teams with a minimum of two members who have a project to demonstrate and a passion for technology and innovation.

### Bring Your Ideas to the Pavilion

Turn your work into an experience others can explore. Connect with developers, learn from professionals, and let the community see what you've built.

**Have something to showcase? Join the Tech Pavilion at DevCarnival 2027!**
