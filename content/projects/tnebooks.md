## Overview

**TNEBooks** is the official digital textbook distribution and learning portal managed by the Tamil Nadu Text Book and Educational Services Corporation. It provides millions of K-12 students, educators, and competitive exam aspirants with free access to standardized, multi-lingual educational material in digital formats.

### Technical Capabilities

- **High-Throughput Content Delivery Network (CDN):** Optimized edge distribution to handle massive traffic spikes during peak academic session starts and exam preparation windows.
- **Interactive PDF & ePub Rendering:** Light-weight web viewers tailored for low-bandwidth rural connections and entry-level mobile devices.
- **Bilingual Content Indexing:** Full-text search support across Tamil and English textbooks with instant chapter-level deep linking.
- **QR Code Integration:** Direct synchronization between physical printed textbooks and supplementary online audio-visual learning modules.

### Scale & Reach

- Serves **6+ million students** across primary and secondary schools in Tamil Nadu.
- Houses digital textbooks, teacher handbooks, and supplementary study materials from Class 1 through Class 12.
