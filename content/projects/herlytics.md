## Overview

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and Polycystic Ovarian Disease (PCOD) affect an estimated 1 in 5 women of reproductive age worldwide, yet low clinical awareness, fragmented tracking tools, and social hesitation lead to delayed diagnoses and unmanaged hormonal imbalances. Conventional health applications often fail to correlate multifaceted lifestyle variables—such as sleep disruption, stress, dietary habits, and cycle regularity—into unified, actionable risk evaluations.

**HerLytics** addresses this critical healthcare accessibility gap as an AI-powered, client-side women's wellness and health analytics platform. By synthesizing user-reported biomarkers, menstrual history, metabolic markers, and daily vitality indicators, HerLytics provides early risk screening, personalized nutritional interventions, and longitudinal cycle tracking. Aligned directly with **UN SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-Being)** and **SDG 5 (Gender Equality)**, HerLytics empowers women with proactive health intelligence while complementing professional gynecological and endocrinological care.

### Multi-Step Health Assessment & Risk Scoring Engine

- **Comprehensive Health Assessment:** Evaluates multi-dimensional lifestyle data through a structured multi-step intake covering demographics, menstrual regularity, ovulation indicators, reproductive history, metabolic markers, and stress factors.
- **Proactive Risk Evaluation:** Analyzes lifestyle risk factors and symptom correlations to deliver transparent risk stratification for conditions like PCOS, PCOD, thyroid irregularities, and nutritional deficiencies.
- **Precision Nutrition & Diet Guidance:** Dynamically computes individualized macronutrient balances, recommending anti-inflammatory superfoods, meal timing guidelines, and foods to moderate based on the user's specific hormonal profile.
- **Lifestyle & Vitality Tracking:** Continuous day-to-day logging for water consumption, sleep cycles, physical workouts, and symptom heatmaps to help identify triggers before flare-ups occur.
- **Clinical Consultation Summaries:** Generates clean, exportable visual health dossiers and trend charts that users can present during clinical consultations with healthcare providers.

### Architecture & Technology Stack

- **Frontend Core:** Built on **React 19** and **TypeScript**, ensuring rapid rendering performance, strict type safety, and an accessible, responsive interface.
- **Styling & Design System:** Modern, soothing design language implemented with **Tailwind CSS**, Lucide React iconography, and **Framer Motion** for fluid transitions and micro-interactions.
- **Data Analytics & Visualization:** Interactive biomarker charts, trend lines, and symptom correlations powered by **Recharts**.
- **Build & Quality Tooling:** Bundled with **Vite** for ultra-fast HMR and optimized production asset minification, alongside **Oxlint** for rigorous static code analysis.
- **Deployment:** Continuous deployment and edge hosting on **Vercel** (`https://herlytics-v1.vercel.app/`).

### Preventive Care & Future Roadmap

The HerLytics team is actively exploring offline-first encrypted on-device storage to guarantee absolute user health privacy, integration with smart wearable fitness telemetry (heart rate variability and sleep tracking), and multi-lingual regional language support across Tamil and Hindi to expand preventive wellness education across rural communities.
