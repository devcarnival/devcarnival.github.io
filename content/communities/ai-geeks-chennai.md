AI Geeks Chennai is an active, developer-first technology community based in Chennai, dedicated to democratizing Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and Machine Learning innovations through hands-on learning, architectural deep-dives, and community-driven knowledge sharing.

### About AI Geeks Chennai

Operating at the forefront of the artificial intelligence revolution, AI Geeks Chennai brings together AI researchers, data scientists, software engineers, indie builders, enterprise architects, and technology enthusiasts from across Chennai and South India. The community fosters an open, collaborative ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between rapid academic and industrial AI research and real-world software engineering practice.

Through regular in-person meetups, interactive technical workshops, and cross-community hackathons, AI Geeks Chennai empowers regional engineers to build, deploy, and scale production-ready intelligent systems.

### Core Focus Domains

- **Agentic AI & Autonomous Systems:** Exploring multi-agent orchestration frameworks, tool execution patterns, task decomposition, and persistent agent workflows across real-world business scenarios.
- **Generative AI & LLM Engineering:** Hands-on implementations of open and proprietary Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering methodologies, structured outputs, and local model inference.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Advanced knowledge retrieval architectures, vector databases, semantic search, hybrid retrieval pipelines, and document intelligence systems.
- **Enterprise AI & Copilot Solutions:** Deep dives into enterprise AI tooling, Microsoft Copilot Studio, workflow automation, and governance frameworks designed for enterprise deployment.
- **AI Security, Ethics & Governance:** Practical discussions on model alignment, data privacy, guardrails, and secure deployment patterns for customer-facing AI applications.

### Flagship Community Initiatives

- **Monthly In-Person Technical Meetups:** Regular technical gatherings hosted across leading tech hubs and coworking spaces in Chennai, featuring architectural case studies, live coding demos, and keynote talks from industry practitioners.
- **Hands-on Build Labs & Codelabs:** Practical, code-along workshops guiding participants from foundational AI concepts to production-grade model serving and multi-agent coordination.
- **Hackathons & Ecosystem Partnerships:** Collaborating with regional and national developer conferences, collegiate chapters, and tech communities to drive innovation and support regional hackathons.
- **Peer Networking & Career Acceleration:** A welcoming platform where budding developers, career switchers, and seasoned practitioners connect, exchange insights, and discover collaborative project opportunities.

### DevCarnival 2026 Collaboration

As an ecosystem collaborator for DevCarnival 2026, AI Geeks Chennai joins the festival to drive hands-on workshops on Agentic AI, share best practices for deploying production LLMs, and mentor developers exploring cutting-edge artificial intelligence across the conference grounds.
