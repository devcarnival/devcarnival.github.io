SheBuilds Chennai is a vibrant, inclusive technology and builder community dedicated to empowering women and underrepresented innovators across Chennai and Tamil Nadu through hands-on technical skill building, open-source collaboration, hackathons, and leadership development.

### About SheBuilds Chennai

Operating as an active regional chapter under the broader SheBuilds Ecosystem, SheBuilds Chennai fosters an open, supportive space where women developers, designers, engineering students, researchers, and tech founders come together to learn, build, and lead. The community focuses on dismantling systemic barriers and closing the gender gap in tech by turning aspiring developers into confident builders, open-source contributors, and industry leaders.

From grassroots university workshops to large-scale conferences and hackathons, SheBuilds Chennai creates spaces for technical excellence and purposeful networking, connecting participants directly with seasoned engineering mentors, tech leaders, and inclusive hiring partners.

### Core Focus Domains

- **Software Engineering & Open Source:** Encouraging active open-source contributions, Git workflows, full-stack application development, and participation in global initiatives like Hacktoberfest and IndiaFOSS.
- **Artificial Intelligence & Data Systems:** Hands-on explorations of machine learning, modern Generative AI tooling, prompt engineering, and building real-world solutions using open datasets.
- **Product Design & UI/UX Engineering:** Design thinking methodologies, interactive prototyping, user experience research, and building accessible, human-centric software.
- **Hackathons & Real-World Building:** Translating concepts into production-ready software through rapid prototyping, team problem solving, and architecture pitching.
- **Leadership, Mentorship & Career Acceleration:** Industry mentorship chains, technical interview prep, public speaking enablement, and pathways into technical leadership and tech entrepreneurship.

### Flagship Initiatives & Community Programs

- **Code & Connect Conference Series:** Marquee regional gatherings bringing together hundreds of engineers, student technologists, industry leads, and male allies for keynotes, panel discussions, and career networking.
- **SheBuilds Chennai Hack (Code & Challenge):** Intense multi-round hackathons combining online ideation phases with offline build finales, challenging teams to engineer impact-driven solutions for real-world civic, industrial, and technological problems.
- **Technical Devrooms & Hands-On Codelabs:** Interactive, deep-dive workshops on specialized technical tracks—including open data systems, web development, and cloud tools—hosted across leading engineering campuses in Chennai.
- **Inclusive Mentorship & Community Cohorts:** Structured peer-learning circles, resume review clinics, and mentorship pairings that guide early-career developers toward internships, fellowships, and full-time technical roles.

### DevCarnival 2026 Collaboration

As an ecosystem community partner for DevCarnival 2026, SheBuilds Chennai joins the festival to champion diversity and technical craftsmanship across the conference. The community collaborates with DevCarnival to host inclusive tech sessions, mentor emerging builders in hands-on pavilions, and celebrate women leading engineering innovation across Tamil Nadu.
