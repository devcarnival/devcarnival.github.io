Doing Things AI is a dynamic 0→1 AI builder community and startup incubator network dedicated to empowering founders, engineers, designers, and creators to turn artificial intelligence ideas into production-ready prototypes, functional applications, and scalable businesses.

### About Doing Things AI

Founded on the principle that the true value of artificial intelligence lies in execution and shipment, Doing Things AI bridges the gap between theoretical AI models, sandbox experimentation, and tangible real-world products. The community brings together forward-thinking developers, indie makers, startup founders, and technical professionals across Chennai and the broader AI ecosystem.

Rather than remaining confined to conversational prompts or passive learning, Doing Things AI champions a builder-first culture where members collaborate, rapidly prototype, validate with real users, and navigate the journey from zero to launch.

### Core Focus Domains

- **0→1 Product Prototyping:** Accelerating product development cycles through rapid AI-assisted development, low-code/no-code integrations, and functional MVP rollouts.
- **Agentic Workflows & Autonomous Systems:** Designing autonomous AI agents, tool invocation patterns, task decomposition, and practical workflow automations that solve actual operational problems.
- **Applied Generative AI & Model Integration:** Integrating open and closed frontier foundation models, function calling, fine-tuned domain models, and prompt engineering into scalable software architectures.
- **Product Validation & Go-To-Market (GTM):** Focusing heavily on user acquisition, product-market fit, user feedback loops, and business models for AI-native startups.
- **Collaborative Engineering & Code Sprints:** Fostering cross-disciplinary collaboration between developers, designers, and product strategists to build high-impact technical products.

### Flagship Community Initiatives

- **Build & Blend Sessions:** Regular hands-on co-working nights and builder meetups where developers and founders bring their laptops, share progress, test new tools, and ship features together.
- **10-Hour Startup Hackathons:** High-intensity rapid buildathons designed to take an idea from napkin sketch to a functioning, deployed prototype with early user validation within 10 hours.
- **Talk to Build & Demo Days:** Interactive pitch sessions and demo showcases where builders present functional prototypes to fellow community members, angel investors, and seasoned product leaders.
- **Women Who Build:** Dedicated hack nights, workshops, and mentorship initiatives celebrating and accelerating women technologists and founders building in the AI ecosystem.
- **Run & Connect Meetups:** Blending community wellness and networking through fun runs, casual coffee catch-ups, and peer-to-peer idea exchanges.

### DevCarnival 2027 Collaboration

As an official ecosystem collaborator for DevCarnival 2027, Doing Things AI brings its vibrant builder spirit to the carnival grounds. The community will host interactive building sessions, showcase innovative prototypes emerging from their builder cohort, and guide attendees on how to navigate from foundational AI concepts to deployed, customer-facing applications.
