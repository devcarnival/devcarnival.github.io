Cyber Vattam is a pioneering community initiative dedicated to democratizing cybersecurity and emerging technology education through native Indian languages, breaking linguistic barriers in tech.

### About Cyber Vattam

Founded by cybersecurity architect Venkatesh Jambulingam, Cyber Vattam (சைபர் வட்டம்) is built on the vision that foundational technology education should be accessible to everyone in their native language. By curating structured learning resources in Tamil and English—with plans to expand across more Indian languages—Cyber Vattam empowers students, developers, and aspiring engineers to master complex technical concepts with clarity and confidence.

### Core Focus Domains

- **Cybersecurity & Infrastructure:** Deep-dive knowledge tracks spanning Cryptography, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), Identity & Access Management (IAM & CIAM), Domain Name System (DNS), and global Internet governance (ICANN/IANA).
- **Emerging & Cloud Technologies:** Practical conceptual guides and architectural frameworks covering Cloud Computing, Cloud Security, Blockchain, Distributed Ledger Technology, and Digital Assets.
- **Open Educational Resources:** All curated guides, architectural slides, and learning materials are released under Creative Commons licensing (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), encouraging open sharing, localization, and community contributions.

### Community Impact & Mission

Through structured educational guides, open-source documentation, and knowledge-sharing sessions, Cyber Vattam bridges the divide between foundational theory and real-world cybersecurity practice, inspiring regional technologists to build secure, world-class software systems.
