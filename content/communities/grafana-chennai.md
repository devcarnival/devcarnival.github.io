Grafana and Friends Chennai (also known as Grafana Chennai) is an active regional community chapter and open meetup group dedicated to open-source monitoring, full-stack observability, metrics, logs, traces, and modern cloud infrastructure. Affiliated with the global Grafana & Friends community network supported by Grafana Labs, the chapter brings together software engineers, Site Reliability Engineers (SREs), DevOps practitioners, platform architects, and students across Chennai and Tamil Nadu to share production experiences, master telemetry tooling, and operate resilient systems at scale.

### About Grafana and Friends Chennai

In today's distributed and cloud-native software landscape, understanding what happens inside complex systems is critical. Grafana and Friends Chennai provides a vendor-neutral, practitioner-focused forum where engineers dive deep into real-world observability challenges—from high-throughput log aggregation and distributed trace visualization to proactive alerting and continuous performance profiling.

The community hosts regular, 100% free offline meetups, interactive technical deep dives, and hands-on workshops across premier technology corridors in Chennai—including OMR, Guindy, T. Nagar, and academic hubs like IIT Madras Research Park and Anna University. Beyond theoretical discussions, the chapter emphasizes production war stories, post-mortem analysis, and practical troubleshooting runbooks that help engineers diagnose latency bottlenecks and eliminate downtime in their own organizations.

### Core Focus Domains

- **The LGTM Observability Stack:** Architectural deep-dives and production best practices for Loki (log aggregation), Grafana (visualization & unified dashboards), Tempo (distributed tracing), and Mimir (horizontally scalable metrics).
- **OpenTelemetry & Modern Telemetry Pipelines:** Practical instrumentation using OpenTelemetry (OTel) APIs and SDKs, telemetry collectors, and eBPF-powered auto-instrumentation using tools like Grafana Beyla.
- **Prometheus & Time-Series Monitoring:** Scalable metric collection, advanced PromQL querying, exporter configuration, and intelligent alert design with Prometheus Alertmanager.
- **Continuous Profiling & Performance Tuning:** Identifying production memory leaks, CPU hot spots, and latency bottlenecks using continuous profiling tools like Grafana Pyroscope.
- **SRE, SLOs & Incident Response:** Designing meaningful Service Level Objectives (SLOs) and Error Budgets, implementing synthetic monitoring with Grafana k6, and streamlining on-call incident workflows.
- **Kubernetes & Cloud-Native Telemetry:** Unified cluster monitoring, container runtime insights, service mesh telemetry, and observability for AI workloads and agentic systems.

### Flagship Initiatives & Community Activities

- **In-Person Technical Meetups:** Free gatherings featuring technical keynotes, architecture reviews, and candid lightning talks from local engineering leaders and open-source practitioners.
- **Hands-On Observability Workshops:** Step-by-step codelabs guiding developers through instrumenting distributed microservices, building actionable dashboards, and setting up end-to-end tracing pipelines.
- **Production War Stories & Runbooks:** Blameless discussions where practitioners unpack past system outages, share real diagnostic traces, and break down the observability strategies that resolved them.
- **Student & Career Enablement:** Mentoring aspiring engineers, cloud newcomers, and students to build foundational SRE and observability skill sets, preparing them for modern platform engineering careers.
