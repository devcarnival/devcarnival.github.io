The **AI-SEC Community** (AI-SEC Community Foundation) is an independent, practitioner-led non-profit organization dedicated to advancing artificial intelligence security, adversarial resilience, and cybersecurity awareness. Operating at the frontier of technology and trust, the community focuses on two interconnected pillars: **Securing AI Systems** (defending AI pipelines, foundation models, and agentic workflows) and **AI for Security** (leveraging machine learning and automation to fortify cyber defense).

### About AI-SEC Community

Founded by cybersecurity veteran **Micheal Joshuva** (Founder & CEO of MICH JOSH CYBERSECURITY), the AI-SEC Community bridges the critical gap between rapid artificial intelligence adoption and enterprise security governance. The community unites cybersecurity researchers, CISOs, penetration testers, DevSecOps practitioners, data scientists, and students across Chennai, Bengaluru, and the broader technology ecosystem.

Through monthly technical meetups, hands-on vulnerability labs, conference tracks, and executive roundtables, the community establishes open, pragmatic frameworks to help organizations assess, threat model, and secure AI systems before deploying them into mission-critical environments.

### Core Focus Domains

- **LLM & Generative AI Security:** Analyzing and mitigating emerging threats across Large Language Models, including prompt injection, jailbreaking, training data poisoning, model extraction, and insecure output handling mapped to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications.
- **Adversarial Machine Learning:** Exploring adversarial evasion techniques, feature collision attacks, and backdoor triggers using open-source toolkits such as the IBM Adversarial Robustness Toolbox (ART).
- **Agentic AI & Autonomous Systems Defense:** Threat modeling autonomous multi-agent networks, tool calling authorization, Model Context Protocol (MCP) security boundaries, and zero-trust execution sandboxes.
- **AI Supply Chain & Data Provenance:** Auditing foundation model checkpoints, securing training and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, and verifying dataset integrity across distributed architectures.
- **AI-Powered Cyber Defense & SecOps:** Harnessing machine learning models for intelligent threat hunting, automated anomaly detection, adaptive vulnerability assessment (VAPT), and dynamic incident response.

### Flagship Initiatives & Programs

- **Monthly AI-SEC Summits & Technical Meetups:** In-depth technical gatherings featuring live attack simulations, product security teardowns, and architectural discussions led by industry security leaders and researchers.
- **Hands-On AI Red Teaming & Defense Workshops:** Interactive codelabs where participants simulate data poisoning and backdoor attacks to evaluate and deploy effective counter-measures.
- **Community CTFs & Vulnerability Challenges:** Practitioner-focused capture-the-flag competitions testing skills in jailbreaking guardrails, extracting hidden model parameters, and auditing AI pipelines.
- **Ecosystem Mentorship & Student Empowerment:** Mentoring aspiring cybersecurity professionals and collegiate engineers through real-world security projects, open-source tooling, and industry career guidance.

### DevCarnival 2027 Collaboration

As an official ecosystem community partner for DevCarnival 2027, the AI-SEC Community curates deep-dive sessions, adversarial security workshops, and live data poisoning demonstrations—empowering conference participants to understand modern AI attack vectors and build trustworthy, resilient systems.
