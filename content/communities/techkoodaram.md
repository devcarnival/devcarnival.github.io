techKoodaram is a grassroots technology community rooted in South Tamil Nadu, dedicated to bridging the gap between talent and access through hands-on learning, collaborative building, and open knowledge sharing.


Founded on the conviction that **talent exists everywhere, but access doesn't**, techKoodaram creates an open, welcoming, and inclusive platform for developers, engineering students, educators, and technology enthusiasts. The community fosters regional tech ecosystem development across towns from Sivakasi to Tenkasi, including Rajapalayam, Srivilliputhur, Sankarankovil, and Kovilpatti.
