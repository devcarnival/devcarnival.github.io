techKoodaram is a grassroots technology community rooted in South Tamil Nadu, dedicated to bridging the gap between talent and access through hands-on learning, collaborative building, and open knowledge sharing.

### About techKoodaram

Founded on the conviction that **talent exists everywhere, but access doesn't**, techKoodaram creates an open, welcoming, and inclusive platform for developers, engineering students, educators, and technology enthusiasts. The community fosters regional tech ecosystem development across towns from Sivakasi to Tenkasi, including Rajapalayam, Srivilliputhur, Sankarankovil, and Kovilpatti.

### Four Pillars of the Koodaram

- **Learn:** Practical workshops, study cohorts, and community mentorship across all skill levels—empowering beginners and experienced practitioners alike without barriers or gatekeeping.
- **Build:** Regional Build Days, hackathons, open-source projects, and collaborative experiments turning ideas into working software.
- **Share:** Lightning talks, technical blogs, and open discussions that give a voice to regional developers and amplify local engineering stories.
- **Belong:** A safe, supportive space where curiosity is nurtured, peer connections are built, and no question is considered too simple.

### Regional Footprint & Initiatives

Through flagship initiatives like the **techKoodaram Build Day**, college meetups, and open-source contribution drives, techKoodaram empowers engineers and students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns with the tools, mentorship, and industry exposure needed to build world-class technology solutions.
