Google Developer Group Chennai (GDG Chennai) is an official, volunteer-driven developer community that brings together developers, software engineers, designers, students, and tech entrepreneurs across Chennai and Tamil Nadu to learn, build, and grow with modern technology.

### About GDG Chennai

Originally established in 2010 as a Google Technology User Group (GTUG), GDG Chennai is one of the oldest, largest, and most vibrant developer chapters in India and globally. The community provides an open, inclusive ecosystem where engineers and students from all backgrounds can connect, sharpen their technical skills, and collaborate on real-world software.

Through regular meetups, developer roadshows, hackathons, and marquee conferences hosted at premier technology hubs like IIT Madras Research Park and academic campuses across the city, GDG Chennai bridges the gap between grassroots technologists and global developer innovations.

### Core Focus Domains

- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:** Practical implementations with Gemini models, Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, TensorFlow, and open-source generative AI toolkits.
- **Modern Web Technologies:** Web standards, Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), Angular, WebAssembly, performance profiling, and accessible web application design.
- **Mobile & Multi-Platform Engineering:** Native Android architecture, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform, and cross-platform UI development with Flutter.
- **Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps:** Distributed systems on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), serverless architectures, container orchestration with Kubernetes, and cloud security.
- **Open Source & Software Craftsmanship:** Promoting open-source contributions, engineering best practices, design systems, and inclusive developer communities.

### Flagship Initiatives & Programs

- **DevFest Chennai:** The chapter's marquee annual developer festival and one of Tamil Nadu's largest tech gatherings, featuring multi-track sessions, hands-on codelabs, keynotes from Google Developer Experts (GDEs) and industry leaders, and technology showcases.
- **Google I/O Extended Chennai:** The annual regional edition decoding major announcements from Google I/O, exploring the latest in developer tooling, AI, Android, and Cloud.
- **Build with AI & Study Jams:** Interactive workshops and code-along hackathons designed to equip developers with hands-on experience using modern AI APIs, LLM agents, and prompt engineering.
- **Women Techmakers (WTM) Chennai:** An active chapter dedicated to empowering women technologists through dedicated conferences (such as International Women's Day celebrations), technical mentorship, and leadership development.
- **Collegiate Enablement & GDG on Campus:** Mentoring and partnering with student-led campus chapters across Chennai institutions (including Chennai Institute of Technology, VIT Chennai, Anna University, and SRM) to guide aspiring engineers into active industry contributors.

### DevCarnival 2026 Collaboration

As an ecosystem community partner for DevCarnival 2026, Google Developer Group Chennai collaborates to foster regional engineering talent, deliver deep-dive sessions across modern web, cloud, and generative AI ecosystems, and connect Chennai's developer community with the wider tech festival.
