Changemakers are visionary professors and academic leaders who actively write code, build real-world systems, and understand the evolving demands of both high-standard academia and modern technology industries.

Through competitive **Professor Hackathons**, structured **Corporate Feedback Loops**, and direct partnerships with the **Tamil Nadu Startup Ecosystem (TN-Startup)**, DevCarnival is engaging an elite cohort of **up to 90 professors nationwide** to catalyze purpose-driven engineering education across India.
