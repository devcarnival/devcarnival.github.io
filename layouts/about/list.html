About DevCarnival

Inspired by the spirit of a true carnival, DevCarnival is a two-day global tech festival in Chennai uniting industry, developers, and academia for purpose-oriented engineering.

// why we exist

Our Mission

To bring together developers, technology communities, students, researchers, and industry professionals into a single shared learning ecosystem — bridging real-world insights, fostering mentorship, and elevating engineering craft across campuses and companies alike.

// where we're headed

Our Vision

Two days uniting developers, technology communities, students, researchers, and industry leaders — connecting learning to practice. Through initiatives like Changemakers, we're engaging an elite cohort of professors nationwide to catalyze purpose-driven engineering education across India, so the next generation of builders learns by doing, not just by reading.

// who's behind it

Built by Volunteers

DevCarnival is organized by a network of volunteer teams — a Core Organizing Committee handling festival execution and venue coordination, an Academia team bridging theory and practice across campuses, an AI team curating practical AI-adoption strategy, a Corporate Connect team building industry partnerships, an Open Source team growing grassroots contribution across India, a Tech Pavilion team incubating real prototypes, and a Technical Review Committee vetting every CFP submission for engineering depth.
