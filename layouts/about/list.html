About DevCarnival

DevCarnival is a community-driven technology conference inspired by the spirit of village carnivals, where people from diverse backgrounds come together with a shared purpose to learn, connect, and celebrate.

DevCarnival is a Purpose-Driven Movement

Built on foundational principles to democratize technology, empower community choice, and champion active creation over passive participation.

Universal Tech Access

Democratizing technology across Academia, Industry, and Research. If you have the passion, we guarantee the access.

Community-Led Agenda

No top-down agenda. The community decides what matters, driving real conversations and meaningful learning.

Action Over Attendance

Participation matters far more than just showing up. Build, showcase, collaborate, and contribute directly to the ecosystem.

Mission

To build an inclusive and accessible technology community that empowers individuals to learn, share, collaborate, and contribute regardless of their background, experience, or location. Through knowledge sharing, community engagement, and open collaboration, DevCarnival aims to create opportunities for people to grow professionally while fostering a culture of innovation, mentorship, and collective progress.

Vision

To become a globally recognized community-driven technology movement that connects people through purpose, inspires lifelong learning, and creates lasting impact through technology and collaboration. We envision a future where every individual has the opportunity to participate in the technology ecosystem, contribute their unique perspectives, and help shape solutions that benefit society, communities, and future generations.
