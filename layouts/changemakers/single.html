Core Framework

01 / IDENTIFY

Professor Hackathons

Organizing exclusive, high-intensity hackathons designed specifically for professors to showcase hands-on coding ability, architecture skills, and innovative software solutions.

02 / SELECT

Max 90 Selected Professors

Curating a premier national cohort of up to 90 exceptional faculty from institutions across India, fostering continuous peer collaboration and high-impact curriculum co-creation.

03 / ENABLE

Corporate Feedback Loops

Connecting professors with enterprise leaders and engineering architects who provide direct industry feedback, real-world constraints, and validation on hackathon projects.

04 / INCUBATE

TN-Startup Connectivity

Bridging standout ideas and faculty-led research with the Tamil Nadu Startup Ecosystem (TN-Startup / StartupTN) for incubation, mentorship, and venture development.

The Engagement Journey

1. Hackathon Entry

Hands-on coding challenges evaluating real problem-solving and software development craft.

2. Industry Review

Direct engagement with corporate architects for code reviews, architecture critique, and feedback.

3. Startup Bridge

Connecting viable innovations with TN-Startup pipelines, accelerators, and incubation.

4. Campus Impact

Cascading practical, industry-aligned engineering practices to thousands of students.
